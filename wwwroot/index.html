The solution

Just say what
you mean.

Type your question in plain language. We translate it into SQL, run it, and give you the answer — in seconds, not hours.

You
Show me all IT employees earning more than 50 000
Generated SQL
SELECT e.*, d.name FROM employees e
JOIN departments d ON e.dept_id = d.id
WHERE d.name = 'IT' AND e.salary > 50000
You
Which department has the highest average salary?
Generated SQL
SELECT d.name, AVG(e.salary) avg FROM departments d
JOIN employees e ON d.id = e.dept_id
GROUP BY d.name ORDER BY avg DESC LIMIT 1
